## 🎨 VISUAL CONTENT GUIDELINES

### Photo Requirements
- **Resolution**: Minimum 1080x1080px (square), 1080x1350px (portrait)
- **Format**: JPG for photos, PNG for graphics with text
- **Lighting**: Bright, natural lighting preferred
- **Composition**: Rule of thirds, clean backgrounds
- **Branding**: Consistent use of royal blue (#2563eb)

### Video Specifications
- **Reels**: 9:16 aspect ratio, 15-30 seconds, MP4 format
- **Stories**: 9:16 aspect ratio, 15 seconds each, MP4/MOV
- **Feed Videos**: 1:1 or 4:5 aspect ratio, 30-60 seconds
- **Quality**: Minimum 720p, prefer 1080p

### Text Overlays
- **Font**: Clean, readable Arabic fonts (e.g., Cairo, Tajawal)
- **Color**: High contrast (white on dark, dark on light)
- **Size**: Large enough to read on mobile
- **Animation**: Subtle, professional

## 💡 BEST PRACTICES & TIPS

### Content Creation
1. **Batch create content**: Dedicate 1 day/week to create 7-10 posts
2. **Use scheduling tools**: Buffer, Later, or Meta Business Suite
3. **Repurpose content**: Turn blog posts into carousels, videos into Reels
4. **User-generated content**: Encourage customers to tag you
5. **Consistency over perfection**: Regular posting beats perfect posts

### Engagement Strategy
1. **Respond quickly**: Reply to comments within 2 hours
2. **Ask questions**: End captions with engaging questions
3. **Use calls-to-action**: "Tag a friend", "Share this", "Book now"
4. **Story interactions**: Use polls, questions, quizzes
5. **Community building**: Feature customer content, thank followers

### SEO Tactics
1. **Mobile optimization**: Ensure website is mobile-friendly
2. **Local schema**: Implement LocalBusiness structured data ✓
3. **Regular blogging**: Publish 2-3 posts/week
4. **Internal linking**: Link related service pages
5. **Image optimization**: Compress images, add ALT text
6. **Load speed**: Keep site fast (<3 seconds)

### Hashtag Strategy
1. **Mix sizes**: Use big (1M+), medium (100K-1M), small (10K-100K) hashtags
2. **Rotate sets**: Don't use exact same 30 every time
3. **Research trends**: Check Instagram search weekly
4. **Location tags**: Always use location hashtags
5. **Create branded**: #RoyalCleaningUAE for customers to use
