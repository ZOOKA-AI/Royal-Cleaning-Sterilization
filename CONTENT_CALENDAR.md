## 📸 CONTENT TYPES & FREQUENCY

### Instagram Feed Posts (1-2 per day)
**Mix**:
- 30% Educational (tips, how-tos, advice)
- 25% Promotional (services, offers, packages)
- 20% Social Proof (testimonials, reviews, ratings)
- 15% Behind-the-Scenes (team, process, equipment)
- 10% Engagement (questions, polls, contests)

### Instagram Stories (3-5 per day)
**Content Ideas**:
- Quick cleaning tips (15-second videos)
- Team at work snippets
- Before/After quick reveals
- Customer shoutouts
- Polls and questions
- Behind-the-scenes moments
- Service reminders
- Limited-time offers
- Countdown timers for promotions

### Instagram Reels (3-4 per week)
**Trending Formats**:
- 15-30 second before/after transformations
- Cleaning hacks with trending audio
- Team choreography/fun moments
- Time-lapse cleaning videos
- Educational "Did you know?" series
- Customer reaction videos
- Product/equipment showcases

### Carousel Posts (2-3 per week)
**Topics**:
- 5-10 cleaning tips series
- Service package breakdowns
- Before/After multi-angle views
- Customer journey stories
- Seasonal checklists
- FAQs answered
- Team member introductions

## 🎯 ENGAGEMENT TACTICS

### Daily Engagement Actions (30-60 minutes)
1. **Respond to all comments** within 2 hours
2. **Reply to all DMs** within 1 hour during business hours
3. **Like and comment** on 20-30 posts from:
   - Followers
   - Local UAE accounts
   - Potential customers
   - Related businesses
   - Target influencers (@al.maiiith, @dolls__makeup)

### Weekly Engagement Tasks
- Feature 2-3 customer posts on your story
- Reshare positive reviews
- Host a Q&A session (Stories or Live)
- Engage with local community hashtags
- Comment on influencer posts strategically

### Monthly Engagement Initiatives  
- Run a contest or giveaway
- Host an Instagram Live session
- Create a collaborative post with influencers
- Launch a user-generated content campaign
- Compile and share customer testimonial reel
